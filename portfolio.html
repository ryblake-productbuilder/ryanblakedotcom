Lapse Job

Improved “Lapse” (Removal of restriction on stock award) job performance metrics. Even as the number of stock awards for one client grew, overall processing time greatly reduced and the records processed per minute greatly increased.

  • Approximately 40,000 more records were awarded and processed, a 36% increase from the prior award cycle.
  • Total time went from 230 minutes down to 37, an 84% reduction.
  • Records processed per minute went from 454 to 3,895, a 757% increase.
