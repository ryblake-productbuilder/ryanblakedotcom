Privacy Policy

How ryanblake.com handles your information.

Effective date: September 11, 2026

This page explains what information may be collected, how it may be used, and the choices you have when you interact with ryanblake.com.

Information Collected

This website may collect information you choose to share, such as your name, email address, company name, project details, or other information sent by email or through any contact experience on the site.

Basic technical information may also be collected automatically when you visit the site, including browser type, device information, referring pages, approximate location, pages viewed, and general usage data.

Chat Experience

If you use the chat feature, the questions you submit and related site context may be sent to service providers that help generate a response. Please do not submit sensitive, confidential, or personal information that you do not want processed by those services.

How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • respond to inquiries and messages
  • operate, maintain, and improve the website
  • support the chat experience
  • protect the website from misuse or security issues
  • maintain appropriate business, legal, or operational records

Cookies and Analytics

ryanblake.com does not intentionally use advertising cookies. Hosting, security, analytics, or embedded service providers may collect limited technical information or use similar technologies to support basic site functionality, performance, and security.

How Information Is Shared

Personal information is not sold. Information may be shared with trusted service providers who help operate the website, deliver email, support analytics, host the site, provide the chat experience, or comply with applicable law, regulation, or legal process.

Data Retention

Information is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

Your Choices

You may contact Ryan to ask about personal information you have shared, request a correction, or request deletion where applicable. You may also adjust your browser settings to block or delete cookies.

Third-Party Links and Content

This site may include links to third-party websites or references to third-party companies, products, logos, and images. Those third parties have their own privacy practices, copyright terms, and policies.

Children's Privacy

This website is not directed to children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Updates to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. When it is updated, the effective date on this page will be revised.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact privacy@ryanblake.com.
